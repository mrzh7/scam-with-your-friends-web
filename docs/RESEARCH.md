# Research references

This is an unofficial experiment inspired by the cooperative office and fictional-call loop of Scam With Your Friends. It is neither an official port nor a claim to have reconstructed every original mechanic.

- [Original Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4954910/)
- Steam store short description (EN/CN) is quoted in the README “Inspired by the official game” / “灵感来自官方游戏” sections for attribution; original screenshots are hotlinked from Steam only and not committed here.
- [Developer website](https://jataterworldwide.com/scam-with-your-friends/)
- [Official gameplay trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e4BhBUryVFY)
- [Rybolt gameplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=siFj51d0XbM)

The initial research observed a windowed desktop, callers, trust, fictional verification tools, a shop, office movement, shared quotas and end-of-day performance reviews. Public materials did not establish every level, formula or balance value. The browser implementation supplies its own seven-day progression, state reducer, item effects, dialogue rules and networking implementation. These are implementation choices, not authenticated rules of the original game.

No downloaded screenshots, transcripts, page archives or original game files are distributed here. Attribution and links are not a substitute for permission. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
