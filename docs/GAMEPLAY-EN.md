# How to play

1. Start a shift, walk to the highlighted desk and press **E** to sit. WASD moves, Shift runs, Space jumps and C changes the camera. Mobile has on-screen controls.
2. Answer a ringing call in Phone. Listen for fictional task clues while managing trust and patience. Offline response buttons work without a language model; AI dialogue is an optional server feature.
3. Use the matching desktop tool. Completing its verification steps earns in-game money; merely obtaining a code does not pay. Only use generated game identifiers, never real card information.
4. Buy upgrades or supplies in the shop. Software is installed immediately; physical deliveries must be collected in the office and used from inventory.
5. Resolve accidents. For example, a power outage requires the break-room electrical panel or an appropriate item. Some tasks pause during incidents while caller patience continues falling.
6. Meet the daily team quota before the shift expires. You can request an early performance review; the timer also ends the day automatically, even during a call. The last minute displays a prominent countdown. Missing quota ends the run; restart to try again.
7. Progress through seven days. The browser version's levels, balancing, item effects and economic values are its own implementation choices. The detailed current tables are in [GAMEPLAY.md](GAMEPLAY.md), in Chinese, and in the in-game Handbook.

In co-op, up to four employees share the day and quota but handle their own calls. Durable Objects coordinate shared state. Press V for team voice after granting microphone permission; calling an AI character and speaking to other players are separate channels. Cross-network audio may require TURN.

Sound is off initially. Turn it on in the tray to hear callers. The game does not request cloud TTS through its normal playback flow while muted. Recognition availability varies: if the browser cannot recognize speech, continue with text.
